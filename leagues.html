How weekly leagues work

Everyone gets the same two required games each week. Add all seven Daily Arcade scores for each, plus the weekly total of your best other game. Each game counts once: up to 700 points each and 2,100 in total.

Your optional game can change as your weekly totals improve. Missed Dailies contribute zero; Unlimited does not count. Weeks run Monday–Sunday in Sydney time. Joining includes this week’s already completed, synced Dailies.

League membership shares scores separately from your Friends setting. Leaving stops future access to the league and removes your row. These are friendly competitions using browser-originated saves, with no prizes or verified anti-cheat rankings.
