Private weekly leagues

Leagues are friendly competitions based on completed, synced Daily Arcade scores. Two required games rotate weekly; your best other game fills the optional slot. All seven Daily scores per counted game contribute to a maximum of 2,100 each week. Missing Dailies score zero; Unlimited does not count. Weeks run Monday–Sunday in Sydney time. These are browser-originated saves, not verified anti-cheat rankings, and there are no prizes or cash value.

Creating or joining a league shares your display name and this week’s completed Daily scores with its current members. Keep invite links private. Owners may replace links and remove members; you may leave anytime. Membership is separate from Friends sharing. Leaving stops future access and removes your standings row; previously viewed information cannot be recalled.
