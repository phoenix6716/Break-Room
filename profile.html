How the Arcade score works
  • Lowball: your Daily average, already out of 100.
  • Stake: the chips staked on correct answers ÷ all chips staked × 100. Bigger bets give that question more weight. Wrong answers and timeouts earn zero for that bet.
  • Stack Up: Daily points ÷ 31,000 × 100, capped at 0–100. Speed, streak bonuses and mistakes still affect your points.

We average each game’s scores over the last 120 days, then average those game averages equally. A zero is included; a missing game is not counted as zero. Original chips and points stay intact. This is a shared performance scale, not a player percentile or a guarantee that games have equal difficulty. Older scores missing from browser history cannot be included.
