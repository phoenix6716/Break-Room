How the Arcade score works

  • Seating Chart: each table starts at 100 credit and loses 20 per avoidable drama point, floored at zero. Average the tables in your captured Daily. Matching the stated minimum is perfect; native total drama stays visible.
  • Clue Ladder: Each puzzle scores out of 100. The Daily score is the average of the five puzzle scores; fewer clues earn more points.
  • Time Drop: Each first-lock year guess scores out of 100. The final Daily score is their average, including original captured runs. Exact years and accepted date ranges earn full credit.
  • Lowball: your Daily average, already out of 100.
  • Stack Up: Each stack scores out of 100. New Daily scores average all five rounds. Saved older runs keep their original final score on the same 0–100 display scale.
  • Pack: usefulness points earned ÷ the exact best available total × 100 across three Daily bags. No timer or speed bonus.
  • Laser: first-fire hits × 25. Four boards; retries and solutions never improve the scored result.
  • Time Sense: each round loses four points per 1% away from its target, down to zero. Average all five; original average miss in seconds stays visible.

We average each game’s scores over the last 120 days, then average those game averages equally. A zero is included; a missing game is not counted as zero. Original scores stay intact. This is a shared performance scale, not a player percentile or a guarantee that games have equal difficulty. Older scores missing from browser history cannot be included.

Each square shows completed Dailies from the ten current games saved for that date. History streaks and achievements use the dated results still saved here. Lowball normally retains up to 121 days locally; cloud saves can preserve older results. Its lifetime play counter is included above.
