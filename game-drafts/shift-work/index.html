PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY SHIFT WORK

Move the blue trolley to the exit on the right. Furniture slides along its long side; nothing rotates.

BLUE TROLLEY→EXIT

1. Make room. Drag a piece in its allowed direction, or tap it and use a numbered slide button. The letter identifies it. You cannot push through furniture.

2. Make it count. One slide is one move, however far it travels. Your board score is minimum moves ÷ your moves × 100, rounded. No timer.

3. Find your way. Undo restores the layout but keeps the move count. Restart resets moves; any hints stay marked. Hints show a best next slide and cap that board at 75.

Keyboard. Tab to a piece and press Enter to select it. Arrow keys preview a slide; press Enter to commit or Escape to cancel. Numbered slide buttons work by keyboard too.

Daily. Three gentler boards: Starter (2–3 minimum moves), Easy (4–5), then Steady (6–7). Medium and Hard remain in Unlimited. Final score is their average out of 100. The first finish of each board locks. Menu → New Daily test lets you replay this prototype.

Unlimited. Practise as long as you like. Difficulty applies to the next board. Progress survives mode changes and reloads where storage is available.

These are prototype-only local records. Official Break Room saves, accounts and Friends are separate.
