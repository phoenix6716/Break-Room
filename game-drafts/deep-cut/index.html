PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY HIDDEN GEM

1. One box. Thirty seconds. Read the question and its scope, then type an answer. The timer starts when you reveal the question.

2. Submit and keep trying. Press Enter or Submit answer. If it is not listed, you see “Not in the answer list. Try again.” The question stays open and the original timer keeps running. Rejected guesses do not count as answers or reduce your score.

3. Clear typos are corrected. Common aliases and unambiguous one-letter typos in longer answers match the listed spelling. A successful submission shows the actual accepted answer. Ambiguous guesses are rejected so you can try again.

4. The deadline still matters. Refreshing, changing modes or opening Help does not pause or restart the clock. At 30 seconds, your current draft is accepted only if it matches the answer list. Otherwise the question ends with no accepted answer and zero points. You can read the reveal without a timer.

5. Higher is better. Each answer has its own designer-assigned score from 1 to 100. Familiar: 1–19; Nice Find: 20–39; Offbeat: 40–59; Hidden Gem: 60–79; Crown Jewel: 80–100. For example, two Crown Jewel answers can earn 95 and 100 points. Your final score is the average of five questions, out of 100. Ratings are editorial choices, not surveys or measured player popularity.

6. Quick prototype testing. Replay Daily freely or choose one of three sets from Menu → New Daily test. Unlimited shuffles the 15-question bank, then refills. Topic changes apply next question. Existing saved questions keep their original scores. Use Replay Daily or Menu → New Daily test to try the updated answer ratings. Local v2 test saves are separate from v1 and official Break Room records.

7. Feedback. If you think a valid answer is missing, use Copy feedback after the reveal. This prototype checks a fixed answer list; it does not invent a match or use a live AI judge.
