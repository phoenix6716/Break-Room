# Midnight Arcade — Full Retro Arcade Redesign

This pass goes beyond recolouring: it gives the site a cabinet-inspired visual system with a neon-purple cabinet backdrop, grid floor texture, subtle CRT scanlines, blinking marquee bulbs, pixel-display type, chunky offset shadows, high-contrast game art panels, hover lift/score-card motion, and a new “Ready Player One?” marquee + scrolling announcement strip on the home screen.

## Visual direction
- Electric cyan, hot magenta, arcade yellow, acid green and orange over rich purple/navy.
- Crisp square geometry, thick outlines, stepped shadows and arcade cabinet framing.
- High-energy but readable cards, clearer game-specific accent colors, and more visible play states.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; responsive layout remains in place.

The existing pages and their prototype-level flows are preserved. The game area and account screens remain template examples, not a connected backend.
