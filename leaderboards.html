How standings work

Totals add eligible completed Daily scores, each worth up to 100; unplayed games add zero. Daily Overall is out of 1,000. Weeks run Monday–Sunday and months follow the calendar, in Sydney time. All time includes retained eligible synced history and recorded results. Earlier scoring rules and Unlimited do not count. Equal totals share a rank, with no speed tiebreaker. Offline play and outside help mean these standings cannot prove honest play. Privacy
