What we store and why

Guest play and this browser

Games store progress, results and preferences in your browser’s local storage so you can resume and see your statistics. This can include answers, bets, price guesses, region selection, timers, sound settings and help preferences. Guest saves stay on this browser in a separate recovery copy and are not automatically uploaded to an account. Codebreak also keeps owner-scoped Unlimited code history in IndexedDB to avoid repeats across tabs. Unlimited also keeps a separate per-account, per-game list of puzzles shown on this browser so it can choose weekly selections and label replays. A separate browser-local record keeps each game’s current weekly selection of up to 100 unseen challenge identifiers. This advisory history is not cloud-synced and does not grant paid access. Temporary session storage remembers unfinished practice and whether a locked Unlimited tab should reopen; those interface flags never grant paid access.

Accounts and cloud saves

If you register, Supabase processes your email address, password authentication, account ID and sign-up display name. The site keeps session tokens in browser storage to keep you signed in. Passwords are submitted to Supabase over HTTPS and are not included in game-save backups or friend comparisons.

Signing in automatically loads and syncs your account progress on that browser. Account pages and Profile keep account progress up to date automatically. Guest progress stays in a separate browser recovery copy and is not automatically added to an account. While signed in, supported Daily progress and results sync to a private account snapshot, including the game state needed to resume. Unlimited practice is not included as completed Daily results. This browser also keeps guest and per-account recovery copies; signing out restores guest progress and does not erase those copies.

Friends

Creating a friend profile stores your chosen friend display name, friend code, sharing choice, requests, accepted friendships and blocks. Your friend name is separate from your sign-up name. Friend codes should be shared only with people you want to connect with.

Global Daily leaderboards

Signed-in accounts enter public Daily leaderboards by default. We store your account display name, random public identifier, visibility choice and moderation status, plus a private cache of server-derived Daily scores. Existing choices to hide scores are preserved. Public standings show current-rule completed Dailies for the selected Sydney period: Daily, Monday–Sunday Weekly, calendar Monthly or All time. Overall adds the ten current games; individual game boards use the same periods. Older captured rounds keep their original scores in your history and may be ineligible for the current board.

Private leagues

Creating or joining a league stores its name, owner, membership, joining time and invite-link hash. Joining explicitly shares your Friends display name and completed Daily Arcade scores with the league’s current members, separately from your Friends score-sharing setting. If you do not have a Friends profile, joining creates one with Friends score sharing switched off. League standings include this week’s already completed, synced Dailies and show game totals and daily scores, never raw answers or email addresses.

Service requests

Cloudflare hosts the main website, GitHub Pages hosts the development preview, and Supabase provides accounts, the database and cloud functions. These services receive connection information, such as IP address, browser/request details and timestamps, when your browser contacts them. They may retain operational/security logs under their own policies. Authentication emails pass through Supabase and Resend. Support messages sent to support@breakroomdaily.com are forwarded through Cloudflare Email Routing to the maintainer’s inbox.

Payments and purchase records

Stripe processes monthly memberships and individual game purchases through its hosted Checkout and billing portal. Payment details are entered directly on Stripe. Break Room does not receive or store your full card number or card security code.

When you start checkout, we send Stripe a Break Room account identifier and the selected plan or game so the purchase can be linked to the correct account. Stripe may collect payment details, billing contact details, an email address, device and connection information, and transaction information for payment processing, fraud prevention, receipts and legal compliance. See Stripe’s Privacy Policy.

In our private Supabase billing records, we store the link between your account and its Stripe customer ID, Checkout session and price identifiers, the selected game or membership, checkout timestamps, verified purchase state, membership paid-through dates and cancellation status. We also keep minimal webhook event references and processing timestamps so payment notifications can be processed reliably. We retrieve relevant payment, invoice, subscription, refund and dispute information from Stripe to verify access and handle billing support; we do not copy complete Stripe payment profiles into game saves.

These records are used to provide paid access on your signed-in devices, prevent duplicate purchases, manage renewal and cancellation, respond to refunds or disputes, and investigate payment errors. They are private and are not shown on leaderboards or shared through Friends or Leagues. Signing out, clearing browser data or hiding leaderboard scores does not cancel a subscription. Use Account → Manage billing or contact support.

Who can see your information

Global Daily leaderboards are public and optional. Automatic entry publishes your account display name, a public player identifier and eligible Daily scores/ranks to anyone, including guests and people blocked in Friends. Your email, friend code, authentication account ID and raw saves are not published. Public participation starts on for accounts without a previous visibility choice and is separate from Friends sharing and league membership. Your leaderboard name is your account display name and updates when you change it in Settings. We never use your email address as a fallback name. Guest play does not enter the leaderboard. Friend requests require an exact code and acceptance. Relevant players can see your friend display name and relationship status. With score sharing enabled, accepted, unblocked friends can request your completed, synced Daily Arcade scores by game and date, including Today and recent averages. They do not receive your email, raw answers, guesses or unfinished games through Friends.

Turning sharing off, removing a friend or blocking them prevents subsequent authorized score requests. It cannot retract results they already saw, copied or captured; an open page may show previously received results until it refreshes.

Private leagues have no public directory. Anyone holding an active invite link can join after signing in and confirming score sharing, so send links only to intended members. Owners can invalidate old links and remove members. Leaving or removal stops subsequent league access and removes your scores from its standings; it cannot retract information already seen. Existing blocks hide the two players from each other’s league standings and prevent joining a league containing a blocked player. Turning Friends sharing off does not leave leagues; manage membership on Leagues.

Project administrators can access account/database information through provider administration tools when operating or supporting the service. Cloudflare, GitHub, Supabase, Resend and Stripe process data to provide these services; data may be processed outside your country. See GitHub’s privacy statement and Supabase’s privacy policy.

Cookies, analytics, ads and purchases

The current site uses browser storage for game functionality and account sessions. The live site loads Cloudflare’s Insights beacon for site-usage and performance measurement. Cloudflare receives connection, browser/device and performance information through those requests; see Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy. We do not currently load advertising networks, and we do not sell player data. Paid Unlimited purchases are processed by Stripe as described above; Daily games remain free. We do not ask for advertising consent when no advertising service is active. Before adding advertising or other analytics services, we will update this notice and any required choices.

Following an external source, provider-policy or GitHub link takes you to another service whose own privacy rules apply.

Your controls

Change your display name in Settings, or hide your scores through Settings → Saves & privacy → Leaderboard settings. Removing it hides your row immediately from new leaderboard requests and stops new public participation; Friends and Leagues choices remain separate. Previously viewed or copied public information cannot be recalled.

  • Play without an account. Guest play remains available.
  • Sign in for automatic account saves. Guest saves remain separate; signing in does not import them into your account.
  • Download a save backup. Account offers a download of supported native game saves. It excludes passwords and session tokens. Keep the file private; it is not a complete export of your authentication or social data.
  • Control Friends. Change score sharing, remove friends, cancel requests or block players on Friends.
  • Control leagues. Leave a league to stop sharing scores with its members. Owners can regenerate invites or remove players. If an owner leaves, ownership passes to the longest-standing remaining member; the last member leaving closes the league. Inactive membership records and closed leagues remain stored until account or administrator deletion.
  • Sign out on shared devices. This ends the session on that browser, not all devices, and leaves local recovery data. To remove that browser’s stored copies, sign out and clear its site data. This can permanently erase unsynced progress and does not delete cloud records.
  • Request account/data help. See Contact & data requests for the available route. There is no self-service account deletion button in this release.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, object to certain uses or complain to your local privacy regulator. A request may require private identity verification. Do not post passwords, tokens or personal records publicly.

How long information remains

Local billing links and purchase-access snapshots remain while your account exists. Deleting your Break Room account removes those linked records from our active database, but does not automatically cancel Stripe subscriptions or erase Stripe’s payment records. Ask support to arrange cancellation and billing review before account deletion. Stripe and records needed for accounting, refunds, disputes, fraud prevention or legal obligations may have separate retention requirements. Our minimal webhook references and operational logs are not an account export and may remain after linked account rows are removed. We do not promise that deleting browser data or an account erases legally required payment records.

Withdrawing from public leaderboards hides your listing. Its private profile and derived score cache may remain with your account; account deletion cascades those records. Daily standings show the current Sydney date; weekly, monthly and all-time standings total eligible recorded Dailies for their selected period. Retained eligible cloud history is backfilled into the leaderboard cache, which preserves recorded results even if older saves later fall outside a browser’s history window.

Local data remains until removed by the game, you or your browser. Lowball normally keeps up to 121 days of dated scores locally plus lifetime counters; cloud merges may preserve older results. Other game records and local recovery copies can remain longer. A 120-day comparison window is a display rule, not a deletion schedule.

Cloud snapshots, account details and social relationships remain while the account exists unless removed through supported controls or an administrator action. No automatic expiry for inactive accounts is currently configured by the site. Deleting an authentication account through administration cascades its active game-save and social rows; provider backups and operational logs may remain according to provider retention settings. Downloaded files and copies held by other people are outside that deletion.
