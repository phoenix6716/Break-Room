- Prices and the full amount due are shown before payment. The owner reports not being registered for Australian GST; do not describe the prices as including GST or issue a GST tax invoice without confirming the applicable registration and tax setup.

## Renewal and cancellation

Monthly subscriptions charge again at renewal. Customers can cancel renewal through billing management. Cancellation stops future renewals; access continues until the end of the paid period. Canceling a subscription does not remove separately purchased games.

Customers can contact support@breakroomdaily.com about duplicate charges, technical faults, access problems or refund requests. Apply remedies required by Australian Consumer Law and other applicable consumer law. These terms must not exclude statutory rights or use a blanket no-refunds statement.
