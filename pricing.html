A few useful details

Will my purchases work on another device?

Purchases belong to your Break Room account. Sign in to the same account to use them across devices.

What happens when a subscription ends?

Subscription Unlimited and its ad removal end after the paid period. Daily games remain free. Any games you bought individually stay unlocked, and their permanent site-wide ad removal remains.

Do I need to subscribe if I bought a game?

No. A game purchase unlocks that game's Unlimited permanently. A subscription is for access to Unlimited across the whole collection, including future games.

How do payments work?

Sign in to your Break Room account and choose a plan or game. Stripe shows the full amount before you confirm payment. Review our purchase terms and refund policy. A checkout return alone does not grant access; payment is verified on the server.
