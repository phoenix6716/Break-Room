Contact & data help

Updated 13 November 2026

Need a hand? Email support@breakroomdaily.com for support, or use the account and save controls below. For bugs or disputed answers, include enough detail to reproduce the problem without sharing personal information.

Purchases, cancellation and refunds

Open Account → Manage billing to view billing history, update payment details or cancel monthly renewal. Cancellation keeps membership access through the paid period; separately purchased games remain available.

For missing paid access, an incorrect charge or a refund request, email support@breakroomdaily.com from your account email where possible. Include the game or plan, approximate purchase date, what went wrong and a receipt or transaction reference if you have one. Do not send full card numbers, card security codes, passwords or sign-in links. See the refund policy and consumer rights.

Signing out or clearing browser data does not cancel renewal. If you want your account deleted, contact support so any subscription and billing records can be reviewed before deletion.

Game bugs and answer corrections

Email support@breakroomdaily.com to report a bug or suggest a correction. Include the game, Daily date, browser, what happened and what you expected. For a factual correction, include a reliable source and relevant scope or units.

Never include passwords, password-reset links or session tokens. Crop unrelated personal details out of screenshots.

Account and data requests

For account access, data access, correction or deletion requests, email support@breakroomdaily.com. Contact us from your account email where possible so we can help verify ownership. Never send passwords, password-reset links or session tokens.

This release does not have a self-service account deletion button. A maintainer with Supabase administration access must handle account deletion and broader access/correction requests. Clearing browser data or signing out does not delete your server account or cloud records.

Controls you can use now

  • Reset your password using your account email.
  • Open Account to view your account, download a native save backup or sign out. Account progress saves and loads automatically.
  • Open Friends to switch score sharing off, remove friends or block a player.
  • On a shared device, sign out and then clear the browser’s site data if you want to remove local recovery copies. Save a backup first if you need your progress.

Read the Privacy notice for what is stored, what friends can see and the limits of exports and deletion.
