PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY DESK JAM

Slide the blue trolley to the right edge. Furniture moves along its long side and cannot rotate.

1. Make room. Drag a piece in its allowed direction, or tap it and use a numbered slide button. The letter identifies it. You cannot push through furniture.

2. Make it count. One slide is one move, however far it travels. Your board Arcade score is minimum moves ÷ your moves × 100, rounded. Daily Arcade score averages the three board scores and rounds to a whole number. Native total moves is kept alongside it. No timer.

3. Find your way. Undo restores the layout but keeps the move count. Restart resets moves; any hints stay marked. Hints show a best next slide and cap that board at 75.

Keyboard. Tab to a piece and press Enter to select it. Arrow keys preview a slide; press Enter to commit or Escape to cancel. Numbered slide buttons work by keyboard too.

Daily. Three balanced boards: Easy (4–5 minimum moves), Steady (6–7), then Tricky (8–9). Starter and the toughest boards remain in Unlimited. Final score is their average out of 100. The first finish of each board locks. Everyone gets the same boards, resetting at midnight Australia/Sydney. The deck provides 40 Daily sets with no repeated board IDs before repeating.

Unlimited. Practise as long as you like. Difficulty applies to the next board. Practice excludes today’s three IDs and does not repeat eligible boards until the selected pool is exhausted. It resumes in this tab for the current account, outside Daily/profile/cloud scores.
