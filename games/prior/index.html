PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY Prior

Two real events. One question: which happened earlier?

1. Pick the earlier event. Read both cards, then choose the one that happened first. Your first choice is final. There is no timer or speed bonus.

2. See the connection. Both dates, the year gap and short explanations appear after you answer. Open the reference links to read more. Same-year events are never paired.

3. Finish five pairs. Daily starts with a wider gap and moves into closer comparisons. Everyone gets the same Sydney-date set. Your answers and reviews save as you go. Unlimited is free practice; changing modes preserves your active Daily.

Scoring: one point per correct choice, out of five. Arcade score is correct answers × 20. Only completed Dailies affect stats, your profile and shared averages. Cloud saves require activation on Account.
