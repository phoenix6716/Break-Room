PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY Alibi

Small office mysteries. Real clues. One story that does not fit.

1. Read the incident. Something happened in a specific place between the last confirmed check and its discovery. Only one logged visit falls inside that window.

2. Reconstruct the visits. Each suspect made exactly one visit, at one of the listed times. These are all the visits; nobody else entered during this period. Times are exact. Use verified records, sightings and the suspects’ accounts to work out who visited when.

3. Spot the false alibi. Only the culprit gave a false account. Every other account and every verified record is accurate. “Next visitor after” means nobody visited between them; a stated minute gap compares the exact start times. A portrait, name or expression is never a clue.

4. Use your notebook. Mark accounts as assumptions and cross out suspects. These are your own notes: no automatic checking, hints or score changes. Select a suspect, then Confirm accusation. Your first confirmation is final. Afterwards, see the reconstructed timeline, the false alibi and full reasoning.

5. Close four Daily cases. Warm-up → Standard → Tricky → Expert. No timer or speed bonus. Each correct accusation earns 25 Arcade points, alongside your correct cases out of four. Sydney-midnight reset, free Changing/fixed-difficulty Unlimited and private saved notes are unchanged.

Resuming an older case? Existing Daily files keep their original rules: exactly the displayed number of false statements, and the culprit can tell the truth. Finish that saved set as before; new scenarios are available in Unlimited and the next fresh Daily.
