PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY Laser

One beam. Find your angle.

1. Plan your mirrors. Place two mirrors on Warm-up, Standard and Tricky; new Expert boards need three. The counter shows your board’s allowance. The beam enters at the arrow and travels in straight lines. A diagonal mirror turns it through 90 degrees. Dark mirrors are fixed; striped blocks stop the beam. The bullseye is your target.

2. Tap, rotate, remove. Tap an empty square to place /, again to rotate to \, again to remove. Use Undo or Clear to revise before firing. Keyboard: arrow keys move around the board, Enter/Space cycles a mirror.

3. Fire when ready. Your first fire is final for scoring. A hit earns one solved board; a miss earns zero. There is no timer or speed bonus. After a miss you can retry without changing your score, or reveal one valid solution.

4. Finish four Daily boards. The boards progress from Warm-up to Expert. Arcade score is first-fire hits × 25, out of 100. Daily answers save as you go. Every game resets at midnight Sydney time. Unlimited is free practice and stays separate from your Daily history.
