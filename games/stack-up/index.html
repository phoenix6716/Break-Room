PLAYER GUIDE

How to Stack Up

Put five things in the order shown. Check the direction: smallest to largest differs from largest to smallest. Drag cards, tap their arrow buttons, or focus a card and press the arrow keys.

Each stack has 90 seconds. Lock in a correct order to earn 100 + 5 per remaining second + 35 per consecutive correct stack. Daily and Unlimited use the same points scale.

A wrong order costs 30 points and resets the stack streak. Cards briefly flash green for a correct position or red for an incorrect position; keep sorting and trying until time runs out. A timeout costs 40 points and reveals the answer.

Your Daily Arcade score is your points divided by 3,100, scaled to 100 and capped between 0 and 100. Previously saved runs keep their original points scale and Arcade calculation. Daily has five stacks and one saved run per day. The clock keeps running if you leave mid-stack or open help. Unlimited is free practice and doesn't affect daily stats.
