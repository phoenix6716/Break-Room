PLAYER GUIDE

How to Stack Up

Put five things in the order shown. Drag cards, tap their arrow buttons, or focus a card and press the arrow keys.

Each stack has 90 seconds. Lock in a correct order to earn 100 + 5 per remaining second + 35 per consecutive correct stack. Daily points are multiplied by ten.

A wrong order costs 300 Daily points (30 in Infinite) and resets the stack streak; you can keep trying until time runs out. A timeout costs 400 Daily points (40 in Infinite) and reveals the answer.

Daily has five stacks and one saved run per day. The clock keeps running if you leave mid-stack. Infinite is free practice and doesn't affect daily stats.
