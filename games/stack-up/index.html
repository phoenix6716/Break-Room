PLAYER GUIDE

How to Stack Up

Put five things in the order shown. Drag cards, tap their arrow buttons, or focus a card and press the arrow keys.

Each stack has 90 seconds. Lock in a correct order to earn 100 + 5 per remaining second + 35 per consecutive correct stack. Daily points are multiplied by ten.

A wrong order costs 300 Daily points (30 in Unlimited) and resets the stack streak. Cards briefly flash green for a correct position or red for an incorrect position; keep sorting and trying until time runs out. A timeout costs 400 Daily points (40 in Unlimited) and reveals the answer.

Your Daily Arcade score is your points divided by 31,000, scaled to 100 and capped between 0 and 100. Your original points stay unchanged. Daily has five stacks and one saved run per day. The clock keeps running if you leave mid-stack. Unlimited is free practice and doesn't affect daily stats.
