PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY STAKE

Stake chips on your knowledge across five daily trivia questions, or play unlimited rounds.

1. Choose your stake. Bet 1, 2, or 3 chips before the answer choices appear.

2. Pick an answer. You have 30 seconds after placing your bet. A correct answer wins the chips you staked; a wrong answer or timeout loses them. The timer keeps running if you leave or switch modes.

3. Finish the daily set. Answer all five questions to see your final score and share your result. Unlimited mode is for practice and does not affect your daily streak.

Your Daily Arcade score is the percentage of staked chips placed on correct answers. Bigger bets give a question more weight. It sits alongside your original net-chip score.
