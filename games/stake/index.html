PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY STAKE

Stake chips on your knowledge across five daily trivia questions, or play unlimited rounds.

1. Choose your stake. Bet 1, 2, or 3 chips before the answer choices appear.

2. Pick an answer. A correct answer wins the chips you staked; a wrong answer loses them.

3. Finish the daily set. Answer all five questions to see your final score and share your result. Unlimited mode is for practice and does not affect your daily streak.
