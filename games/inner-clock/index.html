PLAYER GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY Inner Clock

No stopwatch. No ticking. Just your sense of time.

1. Remember the target. Press Start, then Stop when you think that many seconds have passed. Tap, click, Space or Enter all work. Keyboard holds count only once.

2. See your timing. Your first stopped attempt is final. Feedback shows how early or late you were, your measured time and your score. The dial stays still and the reset countdown is hidden during timing.

3. Find your rhythm. Finish five Daily targets. Your native result is average absolute miss in seconds: lower is better. Each round loses four Arcade points per 1% away from its target, down to zero. Daily Arcade score is the five-round average.

4. Take a break safely. Leaving the page, opening a panel or switching modes cancels an unfinished timer without recording a score. Restart that target; earlier stopped times stay final. A timer left running for 30 seconds records a zero-score timeout.

5. Practise freely. Unlimited offers Mixed, Short, Medium and Long targets. Settings apply to the next target. Practice never changes Daily history. Sound is optional and plays only after the result.
